LifeOS

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

LifeOS is a personal organiser app with tasks, a calendar, a meal planner, money tracking, chat and calls. This policy explains what information LifeOS keeps, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. In short: your data is yours, it is never sold, there are no ads and no tracking.

1. Who is responsible

LifeOS is made by Rojin Hasan, an independent developer in Belgium. For any question about your data, or to use your rights, email lifeos.app.help@gmail.com.

2. What LifeOS keeps

InformationWhat exactlyWhy
AccountYour email address and password (the password is stored encrypted and nobody can read it)To log you in and to send account emails, like password codes
ProfileYour name, username, chosen look (template and colour) and languageTo show the app the way you set it, and so friends can find you in chat
Tasks and calendarYour groups, tasks, events, notes and reminder timesTo show and remind you of your plans
MealsYour taste answers (diet, allergies, likes), meal plans and ratingsTo suggest meals and make shopping lists
MoneyYour monthly income and emergency amount, bills, and the expenses you addTo show what you can still spend and your month overview
ChatMessages, photos and voice messages you send, the chats and groups you are in, and when you last read a chatTo deliver your messages and show "seen" ticks and unread counts
CallsWho called whom, in which chat, when, and whether it was answeredTo ring the other person and keep both phones in step. Calls are not recorded: the sound goes directly through our call service and is not stored
SafetyPeople you blocked, and reports you send (with a copy of the reported message)To protect you from unwanted contact and to review abuse

LifeOS uses the microphone only while you record a voice message or are in a call, and only accesses the photos you pick yourself. Reminders are planned on your own phone.

LifeOS does not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or browsing behaviour, and it does not use analytics or advertising tools.

3. Who can see your information

  • Only you can see your tasks, calendar, meals and money.
  • People you chat with see the messages, photos and voice messages you send them, and your name and username. Group members see what is sent in that group.
  • Reports are private: the person you report is not told who reported them. Only the developer can read reports, to keep LifeOS safe.
  • Your information is never sold, rented or shared for advertising.

Some of these services may process data outside the European Union. When they do, they use the protections required by European law, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. How long information is kept

  • Your information is kept as long as you have an account. You can change or delete tasks, events, expenses and your own messages at any time.
  • When you delete your account, everything above is deleted straight away, including the messages, photos and voice messages you sent in other people's chats.
  • Exceptions: a copy of a message that someone reported is kept until the report is handled, at most 12 months, without being linked to your account; reports you sent stay without your name so they can be handled; and deleted data may remain in automatic backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.

6. Your rights

Under the European privacy law (GDPR) you can ask to see the information LifeOS has about you, correct it, receive a copy, restrict its use, or have it deleted. Email lifeos.app.help@gmail.com and you will get an answer within one month.

LifeOS uses your information to provide the app you signed up for (a contract) and, for blocking and reports, to keep users safe (a legitimate interest). If you are not happy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority: www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

7. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Every user can only reach their own data: the database refuses anything else. Photos and voice messages are stored privately and can only be opened by the members of that chat.

8. Children

LifeOS is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Important changes are also announced in the app.
