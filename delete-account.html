Delete your LifeOS account

How to delete your account and all of its data

Option 1: in the app (straight away)

  1. Open LifeOS and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down to Account and tap Delete my account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE and tap Delete my account forever.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and you are logged out.

Option 2: without the app (by email)

No longer have the app? Send an email to lifeos.app.help@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my LifeOS account".

For your safety we only delete accounts when the request comes from the account's own email address. Your account will be deleted within 30 days, and you will get an email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address, password, name and username
  • Your tasks, groups, events and reminders
  • Your taste answers, meal plans and ratings
  • Your income, bills and expenses
  • All messages, photos and voice messages you sent, also in other people's chats
  • The calls you started, and the people you blocked

What may be kept for a short time

  • A copy of a message that another user reported may be kept for up to 12 months to handle that report. It is no longer linked to your account.
  • Reports you sent about other users stay so they can be handled, but no longer show that you sent them.
  • Deleted data can stay in automatic backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.
